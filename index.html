📅 8/2 — 酒店WiFi + 日常表达
「在空中」的表达：
• in the air — 最通用（The plane is in the air / There's something in the air 有气氛）
• in midair — 强调悬空状态（The ball stopped in midair）
• up in the air / airborne — 飞行状态（We're already up in the air / The plane is airborne）
• up in the air（引申义）— 悬而未决（The plan is still up in the air）
「未来会有更多高楼」：
• In the future, there will be more and more taller buildings.
• In the future, we'll see more and more tall buildings.
• In the future, buildings will keep getting taller and taller.
• Unlike there, in the future we'll have more and more taller buildings here.
「县级市」：
• county-level city — 标准译法（Kunshan is a county-level city in Jiangsu Province）
• a city administered at the county level — 解释性说法
「酒店WiFi太差无法上课」：
• The hotel Wi-Fi is terrible – I keep getting disconnected from my class.
• The internet connection here is very poor. I've been unable to join my online class.
• The Wi-Fi is so bad that I can't even stay connected to my course session.
• Sorry I'm late – the hotel Wi-Fi was so bad I had to switch to my mobile data to join the class.

📅 8/3 — 酒店沟通 + 语法纠错
「请派人检查开关」：
• Could you please send someone to check the switches and lights in my room? When I press the master switch, it doesn't turn off all the lights.
• Also, I've extended my stay until August 11 – could I check out and then check back in again?
• Sorry to bother you, but could you send someone to check my room's master switch?
「不想重新打包行李」：
• I really don't want to repack all my stuff – it's such a hassle.
• I'd rather not check out and back in – I don't want to repack everything. It's such a hassle.
• I'd prefer not to check out and back in because I don't want to repack all my luggage – it's too inconvenient.
